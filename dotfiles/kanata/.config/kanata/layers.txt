kanata layers  —  config.kbd
============================
Two layers. Switcher: LEFT ALT (physical).
  tap Alt          -> Alt (normal modifier)
  hold Alt         -> Alt held + NAV layer active (250 ms)
  Alt down + key   -> key acts as in NAV below; anything unlisted falls
                      through to BASE with real Alt held (Alt+key combos
                      still work)

Home-row hold-taps on BASE (hold = modifier, tap = letter, 250/200 ms),
printed letters (Colemak layout):
  a=LSft  s=LCtrl  d=LMeta  f=LAlt  j=RAlt  k=RMeta  l=RCtrl  ;=RSft

Keys only listed in defsrc for the arrows: h (normal letter on BASE).

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
BASE  (default layer)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+
| `  | 1  | 2  | 3  | 4  | 5  | 6  | 7  | 8  | 9  | 0  | -  | =  | Backspc
+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+
| Tab  | q | w | e | r | t | y | u | i | o | p | [ | ] |    \     |
+------+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---------+
| Caps | a.| s.| d.| f.| g | h | j.| k.| l.| ;.| ' |    Enter      |
+------+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---------------+
| LSft | z | x | c | v | b | n | m | , | . | / |     RSft         |
+------+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+-------------------+
|LCtrl |LMet| ALT* |         Space          |   other mods unmodified  |
+------+------+-----------------------+---------------------------+

  x.  = hold-tap: tap = x, hold = modifier (see header)
  ALT* = tap: Alt | hold 250ms: NAV layer + Alt held

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
NAV  (active while LEFT ALT held)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+
| _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _
+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+
| Tab  | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ |    _     |
+------+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---------------+
| Caps | _ | _ | _ | _ |<--| _ |down  up  -->| _ | _ | _ |    Enter      |
+------+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---------------+
| LSft | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ |     RSft         |
+------+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+-------------------+
|LCtrl |LMet| ALT  |         Space          |            _          |
+------+------+-----------------------+---------------------------+

NAV positions (home row, printed h n e i on Colemak):
              h      j      k      l      ;
              <-    down    up     ->   (keeps RSft hold-tap)

  _   = transparent: falls through to BASE (with Alt modifier held)
  <-- --> = arrows; the held Alt is released first so you get plain
        arrows (not Alt+arrow). Consequence: after an arrow, letters
        typed with Alt still held come out WITHOUT the modifier until
        you release and re-tap Alt.
  Caveat: j/k/l lose their hold-tap modifiers while NAV is active
        (RAlt/RMeta/RCtrl). Use other modifier sources or re-tap
        after releasing Alt.
