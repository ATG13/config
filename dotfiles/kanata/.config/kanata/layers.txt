kanata layers  —  config.kbd
============================
TWO layers (BASE + NAV). kanata 1.11.0, terra package.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
BASE  (default layer)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+
| `  | 1  | 2  | 3  | 4  | 5  | 6  | 7  | 8  | 9  | 0  | -  | =  | Backspc
+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+
| Tab  | q | w | e | r | t | y | u | i | o | p | [ | ] |    \     |
+------+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---------+
| Caps | a.| s.| d.| f.| g | h | j.| k.| l.| ;.| ' |    Enter      |
+------+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---------------+
| LSft | z | x | c | v | b | n | m | , | . | / |     RSft         |
+------+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+-------------------+
|LCtrl |LMet| LAlt  |         Space          |   other mods unmodified  |
+------+------+------------------------+---------------------------+

  LAlt (physical) is the NAV layer switcher, see the NAV section below.
  All other physical modifiers (LCtrl, LMet, RSft, RAlt...) are unmapped
  and pass through unchanged.

  x.  = home-row hold-tap (defalias): tap = letter, hold = modifier.
        Timings 250/200 ms: tap-repress 250 (fast re-tap repeats the
        letter), hold timeout 200 ms (hold becomes the modifier).

Home-row mods, printed letters (Colemak layout):
  a=LSft  s=LCtrl  d=LMeta  f=LAlt  j=RAlt  k=RMeta  l=RCtrl  ;=RSft

Variant: tap-hold-release-keys.
  tap-keys list per key = same-hand letters + every other HRM key:
  pressing a listed key while the hold-tap is still pending resolves it
  to TAP immediately -> rolls into those keys emit the letter instantly
  (no roll lag).
  Keys NOT in the list use permissive hold: press+release of such a key
  while held resolves to the MODIFIER (quick opposite-hand combos).
  Right-hand lists (j k l ;) additionally exclude v and b: pressing
  those while held resolves to the modifier (permissive hold).
  The f24 multi-wrapper (kanata discussion #422) is deliberately NOT
  used: its virtual-key release feeds back as fake input and tripwires
  this permissive-hold variant into hold on every press.

CHORD LIMITATION (verified 2026-11-13 by ydotool injection):
  Two home-row mods held together CANNOT both activate. Pressing the
  second HRM key while the first is pending forces the first to tap
  (it is in the tap-keys list), and vice versa. Chording a(Shift)+
  s(Ctrl)+v emits plain "asv", no modifiers. This breaks Ctrl+Shift+V
  (and any two-mod combo from the home row).

  Workaround: hold the FIRST mod alone >=200 ms, then the second, then
  the letter. Verified to emit proper Ctrl+Shift+v.
  Physical modifiers are unmapped and pass through unaffected; physical
  Ctrl+Shift+v works normally.

  Proper fix requires kanata >= 1.12.0: `tap-hold-order` resolves by
  release order instead (fast roll -> tap because the tap-hold key
  releases first; chord -> hold because the letter releases first).
  Not yet applied (terra ships 1.11.0).

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
NAV  (implemented, verified 2026-11-13 by ydotool injection)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Two-layer design. Switcher: LEFT ALT (physical).
  tap Alt          -> Alt (normal modifier)
  hold Alt >=200ms -> Alt held + NAV layer active
  Alt down + key   -> key acts as in NAV below; anything unlisted falls
                      through to BASE with real Alt held (Alt+key combos
                      still work)

+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+
| _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _  | _
+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+----+
| Tab  | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ |    _     |
+------+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---------+
| Caps | a.| s.| d.| f.| _ |<--|down up -->| ;.| ' |    Enter      |
+------+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---------------+
| LSft | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ | _ |     RSft         |
+------+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+-------------------+
|LCtrl |LMet| ALT  |         Space          |            _          |
+------+------+-----------------------+---------------------------+

Implementation (defalias):
  nav-alt  (tap-hold-release 250 200 lalt (multi lalt (layer-while-held nav)))
  nav-*    (multi (release-key lalt) left|down|up|rght)
  Timings match the home-row hold-taps: tap < 200 ms = plain Alt,
  hold >= 200 ms = NAV + Alt held; press+release of any key while
  pending fires the hold early (quick Alt+key chords).

NAV arrows at printed Colemak h n e i (hardware h j k l):
  h = left   n = down   e = up   i = right

  _   = transparent: falls through to BASE (with Alt modifier held).
  x.  = transparent to the BASE hold-tap, so it still works: tap = letter
        (with Alt held -> Alt+letter), hold = its modifier. Left-hand
        a s d f and ; keep their hold-taps in NAV this way.

Arrows: the held Alt is released first so you get PLAIN arrows (not
Alt+arrow). Verified consequence: after an arrow, keys pressed with
Alt still held come out WITHOUT the modifier until you release and
re-press Alt. Alt+key combos made before any arrow still work
(verified: Alt held + x -> Alt+x).

  Caveat: j/k/l (Colemak n/e/i) are arrows in NAV, so their hold-tap
        modifiers (RAlt/RMeta/RCtrl) are unavailable while NAV is
        active. Use other modifier sources or re-tap after releasing
        Alt. ; (Colemak o) keeps its RSft hold-tap via transparency.
