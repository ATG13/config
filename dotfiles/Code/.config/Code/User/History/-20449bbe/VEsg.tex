\subsection{Whatsapp for business}

Looked into the different features whatsapp for business has. It could help us automate the lead generation process.The different features are:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Basic Business Information}: This houses all the important information about your business. (Ex: Name, Address, Phone number, Email, Website, etc.)
    \item \textbf{Catalogue}: This showcases all the services you offer to your customers.
    \item \textbf{Automatic messages}: Can create automated messages for your customers.
    \item \textbf{Advertise}: Can help to Advertise your business given a budget, location area, target demographic and time for relatively cheap prices. (Approx: Rs 600 to reach about 1.2k to 6.8k people in a 3 mile area.)
\end{itemize}
